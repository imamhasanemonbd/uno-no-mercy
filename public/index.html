How to play

  • Match color, number or symbol. No match? Draw until playable, then play that card.
  • Stack an equal or higher +2, +4, Reverse +4, +6 or +10. Otherwise draw the total and miss your turn.
  • 7: swap hands. 0: pass hands in the play direction.
  • Discard All: drop every card of that color. Skip Everyone: play again.
  • Roulette: next player chooses a color, then draws until that color. Wilds don’t count.
  • UNO: tap UNO at one card. Get caught first? Draw two.
  • 25 cards: eliminated. Empty your hand or be the last survivor to win.
