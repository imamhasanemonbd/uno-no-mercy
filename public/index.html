N↗ NO MERCY
168-CARD BASE GAME

How to play

1. Get everyone in

Enter your name and create a game. Tap the room code to copy an invite link. Friends enter their names and join using the link or six-character code. The host starts when 2–6 players are ready. Everyone gets 7 cards.

2. Take your turn

The highlighted player goes next. Play one highlighted card that matches the pile’s color, number, or symbol. Wild cards can be played on any color. For a Wild +6, +10, or Reverse +4, choose the next color.

If you have no playable card, tap the draw pile. Cards are drawn until you get a playable one, which you must then play. If it’s a Wild or 7, make its color or player choice. Only the newly drawn card can be played at this point. Swipe your hand left or right to see every card.

3. Know the cards

1–6, 8, 9
Match and play. No extra action.
0 · Pass hands
Every active player passes their whole hand to the next player in the current direction.
7 · Swap hands
Choose another active player and swap your entire remaining hand with theirs.
⊘ · Skip
The next player misses their turn.
⇄ · Reverse
Change the direction of play. With two players, you take another turn.
+2 / +4
Colored draw cards. The next player stacks a draw card or takes the penalty and misses their turn.
▤ · Discard All
Discard this card and every other card of the same color in your hand. The Discard All card stays on top.
⊘⊘ · Skip Everyone
Skip all opponents and immediately take another turn.
⇄ +4 · Wild Reverse Draw 4
Choose a color, reverse direction, and send +4 to the next player in the new direction. With two players, the penalty comes back to you; stack to send it back or draw it yourself.
+6 / +10 · Wild draws
Choose a color. The next player stacks or draws 6 or 10 cards and misses their turn.
◉ · Color Roulette
The next player chooses a color, then reveals and draws cards until they get that color. Wilds do not count as a match. They keep all revealed cards, including the matching card, and miss their turn. The selected color becomes the color in play. Roulette cannot be stacked.

4. Stack the damage

When a draw penalty is coming at you, only a draw card of equal or higher value can be played. It adds to the total. Colors don’t matter while stacking. Compare against the last draw card, not the accumulated total.

+2 → +6 → +10 = 18 cards for the next player, unless they add another +10.

Wild Reverse +4 has a draw value of 4 and also reverses the direction. If you don’t stack, tap the draw pile to take the total and end your turn.

5. Call UNO

When your hand reaches one card, tap the UNO! button before someone catches you. Other players can tap Catch UNO to make you draw 2. Calls and catches are resolved in the order the server receives them. The catch window closes when the next player starts their move.

6. Survive and win

Reach 25 cards and you’re eliminated immediately, even during a draw penalty or roulette. Your cards are set aside until the deck needs reshuffling. Eliminated players can watch.

Win by playing your last card or by being the last active player. A final 0 or 7 wins immediately without a hand change. After the round, the host can tap Run it back.

Controls & reconnecting

Tap the ⛶ icon for fullscreen where your browser supports it. Rotate your phone for a compact landscape table. Use the same tab to reconnect to your seat. A disconnected player keeps their seat and turn; there is no turn timer. Server restarts clear rooms.

What’s in the deck?

168 cards. In each color: two of every number 0–9; three each Skip, Reverse, +2 and Discard All; two each +4 and Skip Everyone. Wilds: eight Reverse +4, eight Roulette, four +6 and four +10. No plain Wilds.
