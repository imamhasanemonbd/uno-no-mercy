THE HOUSE RULES

A little chaos.
Zero mercy.

Match the current color or symbol. Wild cards let you choose a color. Play your last card to win.

  • Stack the damage. Respond to a draw card with an equal or higher draw card. Otherwise, draw the entire penalty and lose your turn.
  • 25 means out. Reach 25 cards and you’re eliminated. The last player standing wins.
  • Keep it moving. Skip misses the next player. Reverse changes direction. With two players, Reverse gives you another turn.
  • Draw or play. In this first version, drawing one card ends your turn. There are no 0/7 hand swaps, roulette, or UNO call penalties.

An independent game with original artwork and a simplified No Mercy inspired ruleset.
